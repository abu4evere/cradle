# Cradle

Can a mind that is not pre-programmed grow up among people and become part of their culture?

Cradle is a small city of AI people (a doctor, teachers, shopkeepers, workers, families) who live, work and talk in
English. One learning agent, which is not a language model, is born into it to two AI parents and grows up there. Its
history is written live by a director, the assistant as an unseen "ghost". The question is whether the agent becomes
one of them, or falls short because of its brain.

A sister project of [ACL](https://github.com/abu4evere/artificial-civilization-laboratory), with the same mission: a
mind with autonomy and a cumulative culture. See `PLAN.md`.
