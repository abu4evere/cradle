# Cradle council 1: the idea (8 November 2026)

The same text (`intro_for_gpt.md`, as one paragraph) went to:
- GPT (https://chatgpt.com/c/6af0f2bf-e66c-83e9-bf1e-4890d6a23dcb);
- Gemini (https://gemini.google.com/app/1bd0df9db6a2b0eb);
- DeepSeek (https://chat.deepseek.com/a/chat/s/fa06cd54-4fa6-4fb4-9e44-aee39df0884e).

Each answer is that model's opinion, not sign-off. The quotes are short.

## Where all three agree
1. **The idea is strong, and a true complement to ACL.**
   - GPT: "the knowledge is outside the brain. Can the brain acquire it?"
   - Gemini: "the ultimate sandbox for the Symbol Grounding Problem".
   - DeepSeek: most social agents are "an LLM wearing a body"; Cradle "deliberately inverts that".
2. **A truly blank brain will almost surely fail.** Human babies come with built-in **mechanisms, not knowledge**:
   - attention to people and joint attention (following a gaze or a point);
   - a drive to imitate;
   - a bias toward speech;
   - memory of single episodes;
   - a brain that develops over time.

   The question becomes: what is the **minimal developmental architecture** that lets a non-LLM mind enter a culture?
   (DeepSeek's framing; GPT: "A brain can be born with mechanisms … without being born knowing English".)
3. **The AI people can corrupt the learning:**
   - LLM parents "hallucinate meaning" from a baby's random noise (Gemini, GPT);
   - they are inconsistent from day to day (GPT);
   - their culture is "too nice", without friction (DeepSeek).

   **The fixes:**
   - a stable world engine underneath, which checks every action an AI person proposes (GPT);
   - AI parents react with confusion to nonsense (Gemini);
   - structural friction in the city, such as scarcity and competing obligations (DeepSeek).
4. **The Director is a hidden curriculum.** It can steer the child toward success.
   - **Make it an experimental condition that can be switched off** (GPT, DeepSeek). Keep director-free runs, and code
     every intervention.
   - Gemini adds: pause automatically when the child shows a sign of learning.
5. **Measures:**
   - add **autonomy** and **cultural transmission**, that is, teaching what it learned (GPT); repair, noticing norm
     violations, teaching (DeepSeek);
   - test generalisation in new situations (GPT);
   - **compare the child with itself over time**, and use the LLM children only as a ceiling, never as peers (DeepSeek,
     GPT);
   - Gemini: read the AI people's inner thoughts ("I don't understand what Bob wants") as a belonging test.
6. **Small models, through llama.cpp or Ollama, on a shared server, in tiers:** bigger models for the inner circle, tiny
   ones or scripts for the background.
   - Candidates: Qwen 0.5–3B, Llama-3.2 1–3B, SmolLM2 360M–1.7B, Qwen3-0.6B.

## Where they differ
- **GPT, the most concrete:** start far smaller. Its **"C0: grounded word learning"**:
  - one household, two caregivers, one learning agent, about 10 objects and words;
  - three brains (small, large, growing);
  - a no-learning control and a shuffled-word control;
  - tested on new arrangements without help.

  "The first scientifically meaningful success would not be Bob saying 'Mama.' It would be a neural agent that initially
  cannot distinguish two objects, learns their spoken names through experience … and correctly acts on those words in a
  new situation."
- **Gemini:** reward imitation strongly, as an innate drive. Grow the brain by unfreezing layers.
- **DeepSeek:** pre-register the hypotheses before the baby is born. Add a "lesion" brain without language, to see
  whether language carries belonging. Grow the brain when its prediction error plateaus, not on a calendar.

## Checked by the assistant (claims that were new to it)
- **reame** (DeepSeek): real. A CPU-first server on llama.cpp for ternary models (github.com/swellweb/reame). It is
  aimed at narrow, repetitive workloads, so it is not obviously suited to conversation.
- **Unee 2B** (DeepSeek): real (huggingface.co/uneeverse/unee-2b), based on Qwen3.5-2B. **Its own page says open-ended
  chat is not its strength**, so it is not suited to the AI parents.
- **LiveCultureBench** (DeepSeek): real. ACL 2026; AI agents in a simulated town, judged on norms.
- **"2026 BabyLM Challenge, 58M-parameter transformer"** (DeepSeek): not checked.
- **GPT's model sizes** (SmolLM2-360M Q4 about 270 MB; 1.7B about 1.06 GB) come from its own Hugging Face citations. Not
  re-checked.

## The assistant's reading (for the creator)
1. **Take GPT's C0 as Cradle's first real experiment,** before the full city. One household; does the child learn 10
   words by living?
2. **Give the child's brain inborn mechanisms, declared openly** (joint attention, imitation, episodic memory), never
   inborn knowledge. **A "radical blank slate" brain stays as one arm,** as DeepSeek suggested.
3. **The Director becomes a switchable condition.** Every C0 result is also run without it.
4. **The AI people get a world engine that checks their actions,** and parent prompts that do not invent meaning.
5. **Pre-register C0's hypotheses** before running it, as in ACL.
