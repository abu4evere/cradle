## Agreed by all three (taken)
- **Keep scarce practice** rather than a failure penalty.
  - Gemini: a penalty "still grants 6 daily feedback samples".
  - GPT: penalties add reward-scale confounds.
  - DeepSeek calls it ecological but not a clean cost test. It also changes interference: 6 copies a day against 1 try.
    This is stated plainly in the plan.
- **Day 30 is primary when practice is scarce**, with the early window secondary.
  - DeepSeek: fix this before the data, not after.
  - Gemini: also read days 31–60, to show learning lasts after demonstrations stop.
- **Own-experience trust (Cp, Cpk) is exploratory.** With scarce practice it updates about once per kind per 6 days, so
  a null is likely by design. Update counts are reported.
- **Ctk against a matched control, and Ctk against Ct, are the informative selectivity tests.** Ctk − Cb alone is weak
  (DeepSeek, GPT).

## Points from one model
- **GPT:**
  - an **oracle per-kind arm** (copy the expert of that kind only) to show the achievable benefit. Taken: Cok;
  - Cyk should match Ctk's weights closely. Kept as a coin-chosen person's current per-kind weight, the same slot and
    kind (as C2-S's Cy, which GPT accepted). Total copy weight per arm is reported as a check;
  - a transfer test with reassigned expertise, and a no-identity baseline. Not taken: a later rung;
  - a 75/40 sensitivity check. Not taken: a later rung.

  - manipulation checks: per-kind trust identifies the experts, and copy counts and own-try counts are reported. Taken;
  - a 10% or 0% non-expert sensitivity check. Not taken: a later rung.
