# The life ladder: an ordinary life, stage by stage (DRAFT, 10 November 2026)

**The creator, 10 November 2026:** "child to grow up with society have friends explore its world go school sum like
that. Just think about ordinary person life and that child agent should pass the same timeline same pathway just like
humans. Mission is to find out if that child can keep up with society if not find why and try to solve that."

## The rule for every rung
1. **One stage of an ordinary childhood, in the city:** the same events at the same ages as the AI children around it.
2. **"Keeps up" is measured** against two yardsticks:
   - the AI children of its age (the NPC children);
   - a published human milestone for that age (for example the CDC's "Learn the Signs. Act Early." milestones). The
     sources are checked before each plan.
3. **If it keeps up,** it climbs to the next rung.
4. **If it falls behind:**
   - find why: an autopsy that only reads the data;
   - change the brain, never the world;
   - test again.
5. **Each rung is a frozen plan,** with controls and the blind recompute, as in ACL.
6. **The brain that climbs furthest goes to ACL** "with some edits" (the creator).

## The rungs (first draft)
| rung | age (world time) | ordinary life | "keeps up" means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C0 home: first words** | 0–3 | Parents name things; asking and handing. | **C0-A passed,** but it was too easy. **C0-B** is a harder home: fewer hearings, more and look-alike things, tests from day 10. |
| **C1 routines** | 1–3 | Meals, bath, bed; give-and-take games; turn-taking. | It takes its turn in a routine no one labels, and anticipates the next step. |
| **C2 other people** | 2–4 | Siblings and neighbours' children; following a parent's look and voice about a new thing (social reference). | It avoids what a parent fears and approaches what they like; it plays alongside others. |
| **C3 friends and play** | 3–6 | Play with the NPC children; sharing; taking turns; making one friend. | It is chosen as a playmate about as often as an NPC child; it has a repeated partner. |
| **C4 the neighbourhood** | 4–7 | Going out with a parent, then alone; shops, the park; finding the way home. | It gets home, and it knows places by their names. |
| **C5 school** | 5–12 | Class: instructions, simple facts, numbers, reading; classmates. | It follows instructions; its test scores are in the NPC children's range. |
| **C6 adolescence and work** | 12+ | Friends, choices, a first job. | Later. |

## Open questions for the creator (they decide)
1. **Who are the other children?**
   - **AI (language model) children acting their age?** Lively, but they talk like adults unless held back.
   - **More neural children** (siblings, classmates) learning beside it? Fair peers, but slower to run.
   - **A mix?** The assistant leans to a mix: AI adults, plus a few neural children as true peers.
2. **How fast does world time run?** A real childhood is thousands of days. C0-A ran 1,080 days in about a minute per
   child, so the home is cheap. A city with talking AI people is slow (about 6–12 seconds per spoken line on the
   cloud). The likely shape: busy days are spoken, quiet days are scripted.
3. **What does the child sense?**
   - **Today:** words as tokens, and things as look vectors.
   - **For friends and the neighbourhood:** places, faces (which person), voices (who said it) and a sense of
     direction.
