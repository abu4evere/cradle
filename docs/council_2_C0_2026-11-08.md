# Cradle council 2: the C0 draft (8 November 2026)

The same text went to GPT, Gemini and DeepSeek, in the same chats as council 1:
- round 1: the C0 draft (`C0_plan_draft.md`, as one paragraph), asking what is wrong, what is missing, what would
  make a pass meaningless, and what single change they would make;
- round 2: what the assistant accepted from each, its corrections of their errors, and their three different "single
  changes", each asked to defend or revise its own.

Each answer is that model's opinion, not sign-off. The quotes are short.

## Round 1: where all three agree
- **The worry is right.** Arm A is close to guaranteed by its design. Fine as a working base, but a pass would not show
  that a mind can enter a culture:
  - GPT: "a grounded associative learner, not yet a developing general mind";
  - DeepSeek: "a lexicon, not a culture";
  - Gemini: a pass "proves that contrastive loss works exactly as mathematically designed".
- **Joint attention may hand over the answer.** Add ordinary ambiguity (GPT); make sure the arm without it has no other
  pointer, such as where the held thing sits (DeepSeek).

## Round 1: points from one model, checked and accepted
- **GPT:**
  - The **"same stream in every arm" claim is wrong** in an interactive world: the child's actions change the parents'
    responses.
  - Fixed random looks make a ten-entry lookup table; draw looks from shared category features and test new
    instances.
  - Add a **non-neural counting baseline**. If simple counting matches the network, the network adds nothing.
  - Add a fixed 32-dimension control for the growing brain.
  - Use equivalence bounds, not "exactly chance", for the controls.
  - Report accuracy per word and exposure counts.
- **DeepSeek:**
  - A **novel-word test**: replace the name with a word never heard; accuracy must fall to chance, or the child is not
    using the word.
  - Growth on the same days as the tests confounds them; move growth off the test days.

## Round 1: claims checked and corrected
- **Gemini:** "the parents are a static data loader". Wrong: the scenes are fixed, but praise, correction, handing a
  wanted thing and confusion all depend on what the child does. Gemini also called the contrastive pull a hard-coded
  shortcut. Learning by association across moments is a documented infant mechanism (Smith and Yu 2008). **Gemini
  conceded both in round 2.**
- **DeepSeek:**
  - It proposed a new-speaker test. Moot here: words reach the child as symbols with no voice, so it would pass
    trivially.
  - It proposed contrastive-free test trials. Already the case: the test trials are plain requests. Not contested in
    round 2.

## The three "single changes" (round 1)
1. **GPT:** keep word association as the primary (C0-A). Run a separate exploratory **C0-S on social reference**:
   misleading gaze, "give me that" with a point, an absent object, a clarified request.
2. **Gemini:** **remove the contrastive association**, so word meaning must be learned from reward alone.
3. **DeepSeek:** add an **unlabelled turn-taking ball game**, and make routine participation (anticipation, repair,
   initiation) the primary outcome.

## Round 2: the assistant's plan, and their replies
**The plan put to them:**
- C0-A as the primary, with the fixes above;
- Gemini's reward-only brain as an extra arm G;
- DeepSeek's routine and GPT's social reference as the next two experiments (C1 and C2).

**Their replies:**
- **GPT supports it, with corrections:**
  - report shared scenes and diverging interaction histories honestly;
  - arm G must get the same observations and rewards, with only the contrastive update switched off;
  - new-instance generalisation is a separate endpoint.
  - **It revised its own order:** "DeepSeek's routine experiment should come before my social-reference experiment."
- **Gemini supports it.** It conceded the Smith and Yu correction, with two cautions:
  - **arm G may simply be undertrained.** It needs a longer run, or its failure means nothing;
  - the request frame is itself a small routine, so C0 should score the child's part in the exchange, not only the
    mapping.
- **DeepSeek accepts it, with one clause:**
  - **"a pass requires primary accuracy above chance and at least one pre-registered participation measure (unprompted
    naming to direct attention, or repair after correction) above its own equivalence bound."**
  - Its reason: otherwise participation is reported after the fact, "and post hoc is how a lexical result gets
    retrofitted into a cultural claim".

## What survived (the assistant as council)
1. **C0 becomes C0-A, "grounded word association"**, a working base. It makes no claim about culture. All three agree.
2. **The validity fixes above go in** (all accepted).
3. **Arm G (reward only) is added.**
   - It gets the same observations and rewards as A (GPT).
   - It also gets one longer exploratory run, so that undertraining can be ruled out (Gemini).
4. **Repair is pre-registered now, not afterwards** (DeepSeek's concern, met without breaking GPT's rule against
   silently changing the pass):
   - the measure: after a correction, is the next attempt better than chance;
   - it is reported as its own labelled reading with its own bound;
   - C0-A's two readings are "associates words" and "uses correction".
5. **The order of experiments:**
   - C1, the routine (DeepSeek's ball game);
   - C2, social reference (GPT's tests).
   
   GPT revised its own order to this, and Gemini and DeepSeek agree.
