# Cradle council 3: C0-B, a harder home (the question, 10 November 2026)

The same text goes to GPT, Gemini and DeepSeek, each in a fresh chat. Their answers are opinions, not sign-off.

## The question (sent as one paragraph)
Council (Cradle, a sister project of ACL, 10 November 2026). Cradle raises a newborn neural-network child (never a language model) among AI parents and asks, stage by stage like an ordinary human life, whether it keeps up. Study C0-A (finished; blind-recomputed by GPT and Gemini): a small network child (heard sentence = mean of learned word embeddings, d = 8 to 64; things seen as 16-number look vectors; learning by a contrastive association loss on every heard sentence with the parent's attention flag as positive, plus REINFORCE for handing and saying; learning rate 0.05) lived 1,080 days, 20 moments a day, with 10 categories (3 instances each, 2 kept for tests), 2-3 things in view, parents naming, asking, praising and correcting with sentences from a fixed LLM-generated corpus. Results at day 1,080 (16 children per arm): request accuracy 1.000 in the main arm A, shuffled-name control F 0.266, no-learning control E 0.236; novel word at chance; correction did not help (-0.054). But every learning arm (small d = 8, d = 32, growing, no joint attention, perfect cues) was at 0.99 or above by day 90, the first test; only reward-only learning was slower (0.59 at day 90). So C0-A could not answer whether brain size, growth or joint attention matter. Draft C0-B, the same child, code and corpus, in a harder home: 30 categories in 10 families of 3 look-alikes (family centre N(0,1), member centre = family centre + N(0, 0.35^2)); 6 moments a day instead of 20 (each name heard about 10x less often); 3-4 things in view; tests at days 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, 360, 720, 1,080, plus a new look-alike test (the target among its two family members); arm outcome = learning speed (mean request accuracy over days 3-90), end point = day 1,080; a pre-stated 'still at ceiling' rule (A at or above 0.95 at day 30 means uninformative); validity as C0-A (perfect-cue arm learns; controls and novel word at chance; code checks; blind recompute). Please answer briefly and concretely: (a) will this design separate the arms, or is it still likely at ceiling or now at floor - what would you change (number of things, moments per day, family closeness, noise) and why, without tuning on outcomes? (b) is 'mean accuracy over days 3-90' the right primary for learning speed, or something else (days to reach 0.8, area under the whole curve)? (c) anything in the design that would make a size or growth difference impossible to see (e.g., the learning rate the same for all sizes, the mean-of-embeddings hearing)? (d) is this the right next rung before C1 (routines), given the mission that the child should keep up with an ordinary life? (e) your probability (0-100%) that C0-B shows at least one clear arm difference among size, growth and joint attention.
