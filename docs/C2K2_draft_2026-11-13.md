# C2-K2: skills that build on each other, made learnable by a difficulty check first (DRAFT, 13 November 2026)

**The creator** (13 November 2026): "yes go ahead with the difficulty check first".

**Why:** C2-K was valid but hit the floor. No child fully opened a 3-lid box by day 30, so the readings were not
readable. The exploratory partial scores hinted at the effect: with the amount of copying matched, choosing the right
person helped only when steps build on each other. C2-K2 keeps C2-K's question, arms, readings and guards, with two
changes.

## The changes
1. **Step-by-step teaching:** the copy step for lid j happens just before the child attempts lid j, and only if it
   reached lid j. In C2-K, lid 1 was copied before the try and lids 2–3 after it.
2. **A difficulty level chosen by a frozen rule before the study**, on pilot seeds 1001–1024 (never used in the study),
   with **only the arms Cb and T**, so no question arm is seen.
   - **The levels, hardest first:**
     - L1: 3 lids, 1 try per kind per day;
     - L2: 2 lids, 1 try per kind per day;
     - L3: 2 lids, 3 tries per kind per day.
   - **The rule:** take the first level at which Cb's cumulative day-30 full-box success is between 0.15 and 0.70.
   - **If none qualifies, stop.**

## Everything else as C2-K
- **Experts:** 0.85 against 0.25 per lid; the demonstration stops at the first wrong lid; half the results are hidden.
- **Arms:** T, I, Cb, Ctk, Cyk, Cok, Cr.
- **Conditions:** G (cumulative) and U (flat).
- **Children:** 48 per condition.
- **Readings:**
  - primary: Ctk − Cyk in G at day 30;
  - Cok − Cb in G;
  - Ctk − Cb in G;
  - the interaction (Cok − Cb)G − (Cok − Cb)U.
- **Guards:** the floor guard (Cb below 0.05) and the ceiling guard (T at or above 0.90).
