# C2-K: skills that build on each other. Does choosing whom to copy pay when a wrong step spoils the rest? (DRAFT, 13 November 2026)

**The creator chose to go on** (13 November 2026: "go ahead with the next cradle rung"), after C2-E.

**Why:** in C2-S and C2-E, copying everyone was as good as choosing whom to copy. Even an oracle that copies only the
right expert did not win, because a wrong copy is cheap to undo. In real skills, steps build on each other: a wrong
first step means you never reach the later steps to practise them. C2-K makes the boxes **cumulative**.

## The task
- **6 kinds of box, each with 3 lids in a row.** Each lid opens only by its own ordered pair of acts (4 acts, 16
  pairs), drawn per seed; no two lids of a box share a pair.
- **A try:** the child works lid 1. If right, lid 2 is revealed, and so on. A wrong pair ends the try.
  - **Reward:** 1/3 for each lid opened. The full box gives 1.
  - **A wrong first step means the later lids are never practised.**
- 6 tries a day, one per kind. 60 days: demonstrations on days 1–30, none on days 31–60.

## Experts (C2-E's)
- **Before each try**, Ann or Ben (an equal-chance draw) demonstrates the whole box, lid by lid.
- **Ann is the expert for kinds 0–2, and Ben for 3–5.**
- **For each lid,** the expert shows the right pair with probability 0.85; the non-expert, 0.25. Otherwise they show
  their own fixed wrong habit for that lid.
- **The child sees** all three demonstrated pairs. It sees "the box opened" (all three right) in half of the
  demonstrations.

## The child (C2's network, extended)
- **Input:**
  - look 16;
  - who 2;
  - the demonstrated pair *for the current lid* 4 + 4;
  - result seen 1;
  - opened 1;
  - the lid number 3;
  - its own first act on this lid 4;
  - step 1.
- **Network:** 32 tanh units → 4 act scores.
- **Learning:** REINFORCE (a reward per lid, with C2's baseline), and the copy step toward all 3 demonstrated pairs at
  half rate × w.

## Arms (48 children each, seeds 1–48, paired)
- **T:** no demonstrations.
- **I:** watches only.
- **Cb:** copies everyone (w = 1).
- **Ctk:** trust per person and kind, from seen results (C2-E).
- **Cyk:** yoked control (C2-E).
- **Cok:** the oracle; copies only the expert of each kind.

## Readings (to be frozen; day 30 primary, day 60 retention)
**Success** = the share of the 6 kinds fully opened in a test (learning off).
1. **PRIMARY:** "Choosing the right expert pays when skills build on each other": Ctk − Cyk at day 30 has a lower bound
   above 0.
2. "The oracle beats copying everyone": Cok − Cb at day 30 has a lower bound above 0.
3. "Learned choosing beats copying everyone": Ctk − Cb at day 30 has a lower bound above 0.

**Reported:** the same at day 60; lids opened per try (a partial score); Ctk's trust in the experts.

## Open questions for the council
1. Is a reward per lid right, or should only the full box pay? The latter is harsher, and nearly unlearnable alone.
2. Is 3 lids enough to make a wrong copy costly?
3. Should the demonstration show the lids one at a time, before each lid, rather than all at once?
4. What would make a pass meaningless?
