# C1: routines. Can the child take its turn in a day nobody labels? (DRAFT, 10 November 2026)

**Status: a draft for the council (GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek), not a frozen plan.** Written after C0-B went final
(`studies/C0B/findings.md`, tag `C0B-done`) and after the creator's instruction of 10 November 2026 ("You can keep
cradle go do not stop run both projects").

The rung is C1 on the life ladder (`docs/life_ladder_draft_2026-11-10.md`): ages 1–3, "meals, bath, bed; give-and-take
games; turn-taking", and "keeps up" means **it takes its turn in a routine no one labels, and anticipates the next
step.**

## Why this rung now, and why it matters to both projects
C0's child is an **associator**: a word and what is in view at the same moment (`studies/C0B/c0b.py`, class `Child`).
It has no state that carries from one moment to the next, so it cannot hold "what we are in the middle of". A routine
is the first thing in an ordinary life that cannot be learnt from the present moment alone: breakfast comes before the
coat, the coat before the door.

The same wall stands in ACL. Two hand-played lives (`D:\acl\docs\inhabit_2026-11-10.md`,
`docs/inhabit_life2_2026-11-10.md`) showed that a person *can* build a three-step chain across weeks, but the week's
draw picks "rest", because nothing in the mind keeps a project alive between weeks. And NB2 (ACL, results of
10 November) put a feedforward network in charge and it was **not viable**: it reacts, it does not sequence.

So C1 is the first rung that asks for **memory**, and it asks for it in the cheap, fast world rather than in a
600-year world. Whatever wins here is the candidate to carry into ACL's brain.

## The world (one home, as C0, plus time)
- The same two AI parents and the same committed corpus of sentences (no language model inside the child).
- A day is a **sequence of scenes**, not a bag of moments: each day draws 3–5 routines from a fixed set
  (waking, breakfast, dressing, going out, bath, bedtime), and each routine is a fixed ordered chain of 3–5 steps
  (for example bath: water → undress → wash → towel → pyjamas).
- Each step shows what is in view (C0's look vectors over the 30 things), what the parent does, and **whose turn it
  is**: the parent's, or the child's.
- **Nobody labels the routine.** The parents' speech is C0's ordinary speech (naming, asking, wanting, background).
  No sentence says "this is the bath routine" or "next comes the towel"; a check on the committed corpus must find no
  frame that names a routine or an order word (then, next, after, before, first, last).
- Noise, so the chain is not trivially copyable: on a drawn share of days a step is skipped or two steps swap, and on
  some days a routine is cut short.

## What the child must do
At each step the child picks one act from a small fixed set (hand over the thing the step needs, wait, reach, or do
nothing). Two measures, both from the data, no reward shaping beyond the body's own:
1. **Taking its turn:** on child-turn steps, the share where the act is the one the step needs. Chance is 1 over the
   number of acts, stated before the run.
2. **Anticipating:** before the parent acts, the child's prediction of the next step's thing. Measured as the share
   of steps where the top predicted thing is the one that comes, with the chance level from the number of things.
   This is a read-out of the brain's own state, never a thing the child is taught to say.

## Arms (the question is memory, nothing else)
| arm | brain | why |
|---|---|---|
| **N** (now only) | C0-B's winner, no state carried | the sham: can the present moment alone do it? |
| **R** (recurrent) | the same size, plus a state vector fed back each step | the simplest memory |
| **P** (previous step) | the same size, plus the last step's input as extra senses | memory without recurrence; the cheap rival |
| **S** (shuffled) | arm R, but the steps within a routine are shuffled each day | the control: if R still "anticipates", the measure is wrong |

Sizes and learning rates come from C0-B's result (brain size and joint attention mattered; late growth was slower),
not from tuning. 4 arms, 16 seeds, the same days as C0-B's test schedule.

## Readings, fixed before the run
- **"it keeps up"**: R (or P) is above chance on both measures, and its anticipation is above N's, with a lower bound
  above 0 on the paired difference; and S is not above chance.
- **"memory is not enough"**: R above N on anticipation but not on taking its turn.
- **"no improvement shown"** otherwise. In that case the autopsy reads the data only (never the world).

## Still to settle (the council's questions)
1. Is **prediction** the right read-out of a routine, or should it only be judged by the act? (The risk: a brain that
   predicts well and acts badly, or the reverse.)
2. Should the child's act be rewarded by the parent's response (a smile, the routine continuing), or only by the
   body's own state? C0's reward arm (G) said reward alone was not enough; correction hurt, because hearing is
   bag-of-words.
3. **Which memory**: a plain recurrent state, a short window of past steps, or both as separate arms? (The draft has
   both, which costs a whole arm.)
4. Does C1 need the neural **peers** the creator chose on 10 November (a mix: AI adults, neural children as peers),
   or does turn-taking with a parent come first and peers wait for C2–C3?
