Blind recompute for a study of whom to copy, in a small neural network (Cradle study C2-S: a neural child learns to open 6 kinds of toy box, each opened only by its own ordered pair of acts out of 4; on days 1-30 one of two older children demonstrates before each try: Ann always right, Ben a consistently wrong model who shows his own wrong pair 80% of the time; half of the results are hidden). Please compute only from the data given; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. The file c2s_children.csv has one row per child (arm, seed 1-48): its success rate (share of the 6 box kinds opened in a test) at the tests of days 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 30 and 60. Arms: T no demonstrations; I watches only; Cb copies every demonstration; Co copies only demonstrations it saw open; Ct copies weighted by trust from seen results; Cy is a yoked control with Ct's weights cut from who demonstrated; Cp copies weighted by trust from its own results after copying; Cpx as Cp but the trust credit goes to a random person; Ca only Ann demonstrates (blind copy); Cb100 and Cp100 as Cb and Cp with every result hidden. Early learning = for each child, the mean of its values at days 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 (equal weights). Use a paired bootstrap over seeds: 10,000 times draw the 48 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds for every arm, recompute each quantity as a mean over the drawn children; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute with mean and interval: (1) every arm's early learning; (2) the paired differences in early learning Ca - Cb, Co - Cb, Ct - Cy, Cp - Cb, Cp - Cpx, Cp100 - Cb100. Readings: no reading if T's mean early learning is 0.90 or more; reading 1 yes if the lower bound of Ca - Cb is above 0 (else 'not shown'); reading 2 yes if Co - Cb's lower bound is above 0; reading 3 yes if Ct - Cy's lower bound is above 0; reading 4 yes if both Cp - Cb and Cp - Cpx have lower bounds above 0; reading 5 yes if Cp100 - Cb100's lower bound is above 0. Report every quantity (4 decimals) and the five readings. Run code if you can.
