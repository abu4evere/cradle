Blind recompute for a study of word order in a small neural network (Cradle study C1.5: a neural child lives two simulated years in a home where parents ask for things, sometimes in order - "the X, then the Y" - and sometimes with negation - "not the X, the Y"; then it is tested on pairs of things never asked for together in its life). Please compute only from the data given; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. The file c15_children.csv has one row per child (arm, seed 1-24) with test results at day 720: held_test_first (the share of order requests where the child's first hand was the thing named first), held_test_second, held_test_both (both hands right), held_test_neg (negation requests: the share where it handed the wanted thing), held_test_neg_x (the share where it handed the negated thing), plain (single requests: the share right). Arms differ only in how a sentence is heard: B the mean of the word vectors (cannot see order); P adds a learned position vector to each word before the mean; K the first 8 word vectors in fixed slots; Q a small recurrent reader over the words in order; Q- the same reader with the words shuffled (the order control). Use a paired bootstrap over seeds: 10,000 times draw the 24 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds for every arm and quantity, recompute each quantity as a mean over the drawn children; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute, each with its mean and interval: (1) every arm's mean of every column; (2) on held_test_first: Q - B, Q - Q-, P - B, K - B, P - Q, K - Q; (3) on held_test_neg: Q - B and Q - Q-. Readings: no reading if the lower bound of B's plain is not above 0.20; 'it hears order' if the lower bounds of Q - B and Q - Q- on held_test_first are both above 0; 'position is enough' if P or K has P - B (or K - B) lower bound above 0 and its difference from Q lies wholly within -0.05 to +0.05; both can hold; 'not learned' if neither. Negation is 'learned' if the lower bounds of Q - B and Q - Q- on held_test_neg are both above 0. Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the readings. Run code if you can.
