Blind recompute for a study of learning a method by watching, in a small neural network (Cradle study C2: a neural child learns to open 6 kinds of toy box, each opened only by its own ordered pair of acts out of 4; an older sibling demonstrates on days 1-30). Please compute only from the data given; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. The file c2_children.csv has one row per child (arm, seed 1-24): its success rate (share of the 6 box kinds opened in a test) at the tests of days 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 30 and 60. Arms: T no demonstrations; E sees only that the demonstrated box opened; I sees the demonstrated acts in order; Ic as I plus a copy step; Iw as Ic but a third of demonstrations use a wrong pair. Early learning = for each child, the mean of its values at days 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 (equal weights). Use a paired bootstrap over seeds: 10,000 times draw the 24 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds for every arm, recompute each quantity as a mean over the drawn children; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute with mean and interval: (1) every arm's early learning and its day-30 and day-60 values; (2) the paired differences in early learning I - E, Ic - I, E - T. Readings: no reading if T's mean early learning is 0.90 or more; 'watching the method helps' if the lower bound of I - E is above 0; 'copying helps' if the lower bound of Ic - I is above 0. Report every quantity (4 decimals) and both readings. Run code if you can.
