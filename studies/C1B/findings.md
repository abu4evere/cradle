# C1-B: a sibling. Findings (11 November 2026)

**Status:** valid. The blind recompute by GPT and Gemini is in progress.
**Run:** c1b-run 43146882843; 120 younger children (5 arms × 24 seeds); checks All: PASS.

## The frozen readings: neither social effect helps
- **Imitation helps?** No. Yi − Y = −0.0032 [−0.0070, +0.0005] on early learning (mean clean turn accuracy, days
  7–90). Copying the sibling's praised acts did not speed learning, and by day 720 it slightly hurt (Yi − A −0.018
  against Y − A −0.012).
- **Hearing the sibling's feedback helps?** No. Y − Yo = +0.0001 [−0.0003, +0.0005]. Hearing the parent praise or
  correct the sibling added nothing.

## What mattered: seeing the routine go on
- **Seeing the sibling's steps at all** (Yo − Y−) = **+0.168** [+0.151, +0.185] on early learning. It is the whole of
  "watching" (Y − Y− +0.168).
- **With half the turns, a child who sees its sibling's turns learns as fast as a child with every turn:**
  - early learning A 0.684, Yo 0.682, Y 0.682;
  - turn-matched, Y at day 14 (0.625) is far above A at day 7 (0.509).
  - The reason: the child learns by predicting which thing each step will use. A sibling's turn is a step it can
    predict and learn from, so watching works as practice.
- **A child who misses the sibling's turns falls far behind** (Y− early 0.515; −0.118 at day 720). Its memory window
  sees routines with gaps, as Gemini warned.
- **By day 720,** the children who shared turns are slightly below the only child (Y − A −0.012 [−0.016, −0.008]).
  Fewer own turns cost a little in the end.
- **The older sibling was not slowed:** clean turn accuracy 0.871 before the birth, 0.88–0.89 after, the same in every
  arm.

## What it means (the assistant's reading)
1. **Learning by watching happened, but not as social learning.** The child learned from what it saw happen, not from
   who did it or what the parent said about it. Its learning rule (predict the next step) already turns every
   observed step into a lesson. That is a real and human-like way to learn routines.
2. **The social signals were unused.** Praise heard for another, and a copying rule, gave nothing over plain
   observation. Two reasons are likely:
   - the observation already carried the same information (the right thing is the one used);
   - the copy rule fires on correct acts that the prediction learning already learns from.
   So social learning would only show where watching is not enough: a skill the child cannot see from the outcome
   alone (how something is done, not what is used).
3. **For the five projects:** "learning from others" (scorecard Part A #4) needs a task where the outcome does not
   reveal the method. That is a next rung for Cradle (C2) and the workbench.

**The assistant's prior:** imitation helps 55% (no); hearing the feedback helps 40% (no); continuity helps 70%
(yes); Y below A at day 720 35% (yes, slightly). GPT: imitation 58%, Y below A 65%. Gemini: imitation 68%, Y below A
22%.
