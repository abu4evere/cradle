Blind recompute for a study of learning from a sibling in a small neural network (Cradle study C1-B: a younger neural child grows up in a simulated home with daily routines and an older neural sibling). Please compute only from the data given; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. The file c1b_children.csv has one row per younger child (arm, seed 1-24): its clean turn-taking accuracy (the share of its turns where it handed the right thing) at days 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 and 720. Arms: A no sibling (all turns its own); Ym a sibling whose turns happen out of sight; Yo sees the things used on the sibling's turns but hears nothing about them; Y also hears the parent's answer (praise or correction) to the sibling; Yi as Y, plus imitating the sibling's praised acts. Early learning = for each child, the mean of its five values at days 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 (equal weights). Use a paired bootstrap over seeds: 10,000 times draw the 24 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds for every arm, recompute each quantity as a mean over the drawn children; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute with mean and interval: (1) every arm's early learning and its day-720 value; (2) the paired differences in early learning Yi - Y, Y - Yo, Yo - Ym, Y - Ym, Yi - A; (3) each arm's day-720 value minus A's. Readings: 'imitation helps' if the lower bound of Yi - Y is above 0; 'hearing the sibling's feedback helps' if the lower bound of Y - Yo is above 0. Report every quantity (4 decimals) and both readings. Run code if you can.
