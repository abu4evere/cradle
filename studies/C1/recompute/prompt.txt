Blind recompute for a study of memory in a small neural network (Cradle study C1: a neural child lives two simulated years in a home with daily routines nobody labels, then is tested). Please compute only from the attached file; you have not seen and should not guess any earlier result. The file c1_children.csv has one row per child (arm, seed 1-16) with test results at day 720: ant_clean and ant_noisy (anticipation: the share of steps where the child's top prediction was the thing the step used, on routines run cleanly or with noise), turn_clean and turn_noisy (the share of the child's turns where it handed the right thing), and the chance levels chance_ant_clean and chance_turn_clean. Arms: N no memory; P the previous step's thing; W the last 4 steps; R a recurrent memory; S as R but trained with the steps shuffled within routines (the order control). Use a paired bootstrap over seeds: 10,000 times draw the 16 seeds with replacement, use the same drawn seeds for every arm and quantity, recompute each quantity as a mean over the drawn children; take the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles. Compute, each with its mean and interval: (1) every arm's ant_clean, ant_noisy, turn_clean, turn_noisy; (2) the paired differences on ant_clean P - N, W - N, R - N, and on turn_clean P - N, W - N, R - N; (3) R - S, R - P, W - P, R - W on ant_clean; (4) chance = the mean over arm N's children of chance_ant_clean and of chance_turn_clean. Readings: a memory arm (P, W or R) 'keeps' if the lower bounds of its ant_clean and turn_clean are above the respective chance levels and the lower bound of its ant_clean minus N is above 0; the reading is 'it keeps up' if any memory arm keeps and the lower bound of R - S on ant_clean is above 0; else 'memory is not enough' if some memory arm beats N on ant_clean (lower bound above 0) but not on turn_clean; else 'no improvement shown'. Also report whether N's mean ant_clean is 0.90 or more (then the readings are uninformative). Report every quantity with its interval (4 decimals) and the reading. Run code if you can, loading the attached file with your code tool.
