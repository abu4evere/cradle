# Cradle: can a mind raised among people become one of them? (working name; plan v0, 8 November 2026)

**The creator, 8 November 2026:** "Build already developed city and there will be other ai who do human things like
someone be doctor someone is teacher everything in that world just for that our agent to grow. And our agent will born
he/she got ai father and mother just like our normal life. Main question is can that our agent can be part of that
culture or agent will lack cuz of his/her brain developments or whatever."

**The mission is the same as ACL's** (Bob: an autonomous, cumulative mind), seen from the other side.
- **ACL asks:** can culture appear from nothing?
- **Cradle asks:** can a mind that is not pre-programmed **absorb a culture that already exists, and become part of
  it**?

The two answers together are what Bob needs: learn from others, then add something new.

**Cradle is a separate project.** ACL's rules and nature are untouched.

## The main question
Can our agent, born to two AI parents in a developed city, become a member of its culture? Or does it fall short, and
if so, why (its brain's capacity, how it learns, or what it is given)?

## The world, first version (v0)
- **A small developed city on a 2D map:** homes, a school, a clinic, a market, workplaces, a park, streets. Time moves
  in hours; days, weeks and years pass.
- **About 40 AI people (NPCs)** with roles and lives:
  - families (mothers, fathers, children);
  - a teacher or two;
  - a doctor;
  - shopkeepers and workers;
  - neighbours and friends.

  Each has a daily rhythm (sleep, eat, work or school, free time), relationships, and simple needs.
- **The NPCs speak** a small language of words (symbols with fixed meanings: "eat", "come", "good", "no", names, places,
  objects). It is simple enough that a small learning brain could in principle learn it, yet it is a real language
  with rules.
- **How NPCs raise a child,** as human parents do:
  - feed, comfort and protect it;
  - name things while pointing ("this is bread");
  - show how to do things;
  - praise and correct;
  - take it to school.

  Teachers teach words, counting and the city's ways. Doctors treat the sick.

## Who plays the NPCs
**The assistant (Claude) writes every NPC:** their characters, jobs, routines, how parents raise and how teachers teach.
- **In v0 they run as a program**, so the world can live for many years, quickly and for free, on the PC or the cloud.
- The assistant cannot sit inside the world live for 20 simulated years.
- **Later (the creator's decision):** NPCs that think and talk freely, driven live by a language model, would need a
  paid AI service (an API key and money) or a local model. Only the creator can choose that.

## Our agent
- **Born as a baby to two NPC parents.** It has a body with needs (hunger, sleep, comfort, pain) and senses (sight
  nearby, hearing words).
- **A learning brain: a neural network, never a language model.** It learns from its own feelings and by watching and
  copying the people around it, especially its parents. It starts knowing nothing about the city.
- **Brain development:** the brain can grow with age (more capacity as the child gets older). Different brains are
  compared to answer "or will it lack because of its brain".

## How "part of the culture" is measured (against NPC children of the same age as the benchmark)
1. **Daily rhythm:** does it sleep, eat, go to school or work when the city does?
2. **Language:** does it understand words (responds correctly) and use them (says the right word at the right time)?
3. **Skills:** does it learn what school teaches, and later do an adult job's tasks?
4. **Belonging:** do NPCs treat it as one of them? Does it have friends? Does it ask for help and help others?

## Milestones
- **M1:** the city lives on its own: NPC families for 30 years, with births, schooling, work and ageing. No agent yet.
- **M2:** our agent is born. Infancy to age 6: rhythm and first words.
- **M3:** school to age 18: language and skills, against NPC classmates.
- **M4:** adult life: a job, friends, maybe a family.

Each milestone ends with a written result. The tests are planned before they are run, as in ACL.

## Rules
- Our agent never contains a language model.
- No money is spent without the creator.
- Plans are written before tests.
- ACL is untouched.

## The Neo principle (the creator, 8 November 2026)
"its like creating matrix think about that way. Our agent is Neo inside matrix he does not know about reality and
matrix. Hes reality is matrix and all other people are ai nps and he cannot distinguish".

What it means for the design:
1. **Our agent is one child among many.** Its parents raise it exactly as NPC parents raise NPC children: the same
   routines, the same words, the same care. No NPC has any behaviour made only for it. If the world treated it
   differently, it would not be living inside the culture; it would be visiting it.
2. **It knows only what it senses.** It gets no special access, no map, no labels, no outside knowledge. It sees, hears
   and feels what an NPC child in its place would.
3. **It cannot tell NPCs from itself, and nothing tells it.** The world must also be believable: NPCs need enough
   variety that their lives do not look like loops.
4. **A question for later:** if our agent becomes capable, would it ever notice that the people around it repeat
   patterns, the way someone inside a simulation might notice a "glitch"? That ties Cradle to the creator's "bug = law"
   reflection.
